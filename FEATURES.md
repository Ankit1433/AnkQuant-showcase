# AnkQuant — Product Features

## Intraday market scanning

The intraday workflow screens selected Indian equities for short-term technical setups and tracks relevant market changes during the trading session.

## Swing market scanning

A separate daily-timeframe workflow considers broader price structure, trend and volume signals. It does not simply reuse intraday conditions.

## Signal confluence

The evaluation layer combines several technical observations before highlighting a potential setup. Detailed strategy thresholds and internal scoring rules are intentionally not published.

## Dashboards and signal history

The application offers browser interfaces for reviewing scanner activity, signal records and market information.

## Paper trading and risk context

Simulated position tracking helps explore risk, entry/exit scenarios and outcomes. The application is not represented here as an automatic broker execution service.

## Notifications and persistence

Telegram integrations support alerts. Persistent data services support history and state recovery where configured.

## Scope

Feature availability can depend on authentication, configuration, market-data access and deployment state. Visit https://ankquant.in to see the current live experience.
